    Turn on automatic logging

    LinkedIn doesn't let other sites read your game results, so a small Chrome extension does it. It reads the results screen after you finish a game and sends your time, rank and streak here. Nothing leaves your browser.

    1. Download the project and unzip it.
    2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
    3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder inside it.
    4. Reload this page, then play a game on LinkedIn.
